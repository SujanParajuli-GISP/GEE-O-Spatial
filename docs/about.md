## Who We Are

GEE-O-Spatial Solutions Company Inc. is a geospatial solutions provider built around one
idea: **spatial data is only valuable when it changes a decision.** We combine satellite
imagery, LiDAR, SAR, and modern cloud and AI tooling to help organizations see their assets,
land, and environment more clearly — and act on what they see.

The company is led by **Sujan Parajuli, GISP** — a certified GIS professional whose public
portfolio of notebooks, pipelines, and workshops is available on
[GitHub](https://github.com/SujanParajuli-GISP). That same hands-on, open-source mindset
shapes how we work with clients.

## Core Capabilities

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-satellite-variant:{ .lg .middle } **Remote Sensing & Earth Observation**

    ---

    Optical and SAR imagery, land-cover and change detection, time-series analysis on

-   :material-cube-scan:{ .lg .middle } **LiDAR & 3D**

    digital twins.

-   :material-brain:{ .lg .middle } **GeoAI & Machine Learning**

    ---

    Deep learning for imagery, segmentation with foundation models, risk forecasting,
    retrieval-augmented analysis of remote-sensing data.
