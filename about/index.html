    About GEE-O-Spatial Solutions

    Where Earth observation meets decision-making

    Consulting · Training · SaaS for the geospatial industry

    Who We Are

    GEE-O-Spatial Solutions Company Inc. is a geospatial solutions provider built around one idea: spatial data is only valuable when it changes a decision. We combine satellite imagery, LiDAR, SAR, and modern cloud and AI tooling to help organizations see their assets, land, and environment more clearly — and act on what they see.

    The company is led by Sujan Parajuli, GISP — a certified GIS professional whose public portfolio of notebooks, pipelines, and workshops is available on GitHub. That same hands-on, open-source mindset shapes how we work with clients.

    • Our Mission

      Empower businesses and organizations with the tools and knowledge to harness geospatial technology for informed decision-making and improved efficiency.

    • Our Vision

      A world where every organization — not just those with large GIS teams — can turn Earth-observation data into clear, timely, trustworthy insight.

    • Our Approach

      Outcome-first, open where possible, reproducible always. We favor documented workflows and transferable skills over black-box deliverables.

    What We Do

    • Consulting

      Geospatial strategy, spatial analysis and modeling, GIS implementation, data quality assessment, and location analytics.

      Consulting services

    • Training

      Courses from GIS fundamentals to geospatial machine learning, backed by open training resources.

      Training programs

    • SaaS

      Cloud platforms for data management, monitoring, LiDAR digital twins, and spatial BI.

      SaaS solutions

    Core Capabilities

    • Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

      Optical and SAR imagery, land-cover and change detection, time-series analysis on Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2 basemaps.

    • LiDAR & 3D

      Point-cloud processing and classification, forest structure and carbon estimation, digital twins.

    • GeoAI & Machine Learning

      Deep learning for imagery, segmentation with foundation models, risk forecasting, retrieval-augmented analysis of remote-sensing data.

    • Geospatial Data Engineering

      Reproducible pipelines in Python, PostGIS and Spark; cloud-native data access and MLOps.

    • Web Mapping & Apps

      Interactive dashboards and decision tools, from Streamlit prototypes to production maps.

    • Geostatistics & Analytics

      Spatial modeling, uncertainty, and location-based business intelligence.
