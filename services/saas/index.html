    SaaS Solutions

    Our cloud-based geospatial software solutions provide scalable and customizable platforms for data management, analysis, and visualization. Whether you need a geospatial analytics dashboard, a location-based decision support system, or a mapping application, our SaaS offerings deliver powerful capabilities with ease of access.

    Core Platforms

    • Geospatial Data Management Platform

    • Real-time Mapping and Monitoring Application

    • Spatial Business Intelligence Dashboard

    • Location-based Service Optimization Tools

    • Customizable GIS Applications

    Specialized Solutions

    LiDAR for Digital Twins

    • Design and deploy ML models that extract utility infrastructure (poles, cross-arms, conductor paths, vegetation) from aerial LiDAR point clouds.
    • Automated pipelines for large-scale digital twin generation from raw sensor data — classification, segmentation, and topology extraction.
    • 3D scene understanding models capable of identifying asset condition, spatial relationships, and encroachment risk.
    • Integration of LiDAR with multispectral / RGB imagery, GIS layers, and asset metadata to produce enriched digital twins.
    • MLOps best practices for dataset versioning, model monitoring, and retraining over time.

    Survey123 Form Creation

    Custom digital forms for streamlining field data collection.

    Environmental Justice Mapping

    Interactive mapping tools built around environmental justice indicators and community impact analysis.

    SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) Applications

    Remote sensing data products and analysis built on SAR datasets and their applications.

    Additional Applications

    • Vegetation management
    • Storm and wildfire mitigation
    • Asset inspection and monitoring
    • Pipeline encroachment detection
