### :material-cube-scan: LiDAR for Digital Twins

  conductor paths, vegetation) from aerial LiDAR point clouds.
- Automated pipelines for large-scale digital twin generation from raw sensor data —
  classification, segmentation, and topology extraction.
- 3D scene understanding models capable of identifying asset condition, spatial
  relationships, and encroachment risk.
- Integration of LiDAR with multispectral / RGB imagery, GIS layers, and asset metadata to
