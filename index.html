    About Us

    GEE-O-Spatial Solutions Company Inc. is a geospatial solutions provider specializing in consulting services, professional training, and cutting-edge Software as a Service (SaaS). Our mission is to empower businesses and organizations with the tools and knowledge they need to harness the power of geospatial technology for informed decision-making and improved efficiency.

    By combining satellite imagery, LiDAR, SAR, and geospatial technologies, we help clients across agriculture, utilities, environmental management, transportation, and real estate turn spatial data into actionable insight.
